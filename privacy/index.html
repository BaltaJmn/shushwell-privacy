Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

In short: Shushwell does not collect your content, and has no account, analytics, ads or tracking. Everything you make stays on your iPhone. Purchases are recorded by RevenueCat with an anonymous identifier (see Purchases).

The microphone

Shushwell uses the microphone only when you turn on "Listen for crying" and the shush is playing. Each second of sound goes to Apple's sound recognition (the SoundAnalysis framework), which runs on your iPhone and answers how likely it is that a baby is crying. Shushwell uses that answer to make the shush louder or softer and keeps nothing else: no audio is recorded, saved or sent, to us or to anyone. iOS shows the microphone indicator while it listens. You can turn listening off in the app, or the microphone off for Shushwell in the iPhone's Settings.

Your settings

Your volume, tone, timer and the dates of your free nights are stored on your iPhone only, and are removed if you delete the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple through the App Store. Shushwell never sees your name, email or payment details; it asks Apple whether a subscription is active. Apple's handling of that data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

To know, and to restore, whether this installation has Pro, Shushwell uses RevenueCat, Inc. as a processor. RevenueCat receives each in-app purchase you make, assigns the installation an anonymous app user ID, and stores that ID, the purchase history (product, date, price and store transaction IDs) and basic technical device data (OS version, app version, country or storefront). Nothing else from Shushwell, such as any sound or your settings, is sent to RevenueCat or anywhere else. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

To ask for deletion of the purchase record, write to baltajmn@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Shushwell purchase data". Apple keeps its own record of the order under its own policies.

What Shushwell does not do

No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. The only third-party code in the app is the RevenueCat purchases library, described under Purchases. Shushwell works with the network turned off; the purchase record is sent to RevenueCat when the device is online.

Children

Shushwell is made for parents and collects no personal data from anyone, children included. The only thing that goes to RevenueCat is an anonymous installation ID and the purchase record (see Purchases).

Changes and contact
