Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

In short: Shushwell does not collect, record, store or share any of your data. Everything happens on your iPhone.

The microphone

Shushwell uses the microphone only when you turn on "Listen for crying" and the shush is playing. Each second of sound goes to Apple's sound recognition (the SoundAnalysis framework), which runs on your iPhone and answers how likely it is that a baby is crying. Shushwell uses that answer to make the shush louder or softer and keeps nothing else: no audio is recorded, saved or sent, to us or to anyone. iOS shows the microphone indicator while it listens. You can turn listening off in the app, or the microphone off for Shushwell in the iPhone's Settings.

Your settings

Your volume, tone, timer and the dates of your free nights are stored on your iPhone only, and are removed if you delete the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple through the App Store. Shushwell never sees your name, email or payment details; it only asks Apple whether a subscription is active. Apple's handling of that data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

What Shushwell does not do

No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party code that collects data. Shushwell works with the network turned off.

Children

Shushwell is made for parents and collects no data from anyone, children included.
