Shushwell

A shush that listens

Shushwell plays a soft, rhythmic "shhh" for your baby on your iPhone. When your baby cries, it shushes louder, the way you would, and eases back down about a minute after they settle.

No account, nothing recorded. Listening runs on your iPhone with Apple's sound recognition. Shushwell has no sign-up, no ads and no tracking.

How it works

Tap Start and the shush begins. It keeps playing with the screen locked and comes back by itself after a call or an alarm, on the iPhone, a Bluetooth speaker by the crib or AirPlay. Choose a timer from 15 minutes to 8 hours, or all night; at the end it fades out over 30 seconds instead of cutting off. Make the shush deeper or brighter, in breaths or one steady whoosh.

Listening for crying

With listening on, Shushwell checks each second of microphone sound for a baby crying, on your iPhone. While your baby cries, the shush goes up to the volume you choose; when the crying stops, it comes back down slowly. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. Shushwell is not a baby monitor: it does not alert you. Keep the speaker at least 7 feet (2 m) from your baby and the volume moderate.

Price

Your first 3 nights are free with everything. After that the shush stays free with timers up to 30 minutes. Shushwell Pro, for listening and timers from 1 hour to all night, is a subscription: $19.99 a year or $3.99 a month in the US (prices vary by country). Cancel anytime in your Apple account settings.
